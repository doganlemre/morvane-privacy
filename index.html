Dimwood Gate – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Dimwood Gate ("the game") is developed by Morvane Games ("we"). This policy explains how the game handles information.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information. The game does not require an account, and it does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress and settings (such as language, volume and brightness) are saved locally on your device only. We cannot access them. Uninstalling the game removes this data.

Third parties

The game does not send data to third parties and contains no ads or in-app purchases. Google Play may collect its own standard information when you download the game; this is governed by Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Oyun, çocuklar dahil hiç kimseden bilerek bilgi toplamaz.
